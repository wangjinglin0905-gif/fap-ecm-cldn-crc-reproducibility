# Data use notice

This archive does not redistribute raw TCGA, GEO, cBioPortal, CPTAC or spatial-transcriptomic datasets. Users must obtain those files from the authoritative sources listed in `DATA_MANIFEST.csv` and comply with each source's current terms and acknowledgement requirements.

Small analysis-defined gene lists, sample-inclusion metadata, deconvolution estimates, corrected result tables and figure source data are included to support reproducibility. Their inclusion does not change the ownership or licence of the underlying source data or published gene sets.

When reusing this archive:

1. cite the associated manuscript and repository release;
2. cite every source dataset and gene-set publication used;
3. preserve accession, access-date and processing-route information;
4. do not label the 592-sample cBioPortal TCGA route as an independent cohort;
5. do not combine controlled-access data with this public archive.

Updated 8 August 2026.
